About Us

Established in 2009

Khalsa College of Engineering & Technology (KCET), Amritsar, is a premier institute established in 2009 under the aegis of the Khalsa College Managing Committee, Amritsar. Located in the holy city of Amritsar, Punjab, we are dedicated to providing quality technical education.


Our campus fosters an environment of innovation, discipline, and moral values, ensuring that every student graduates not just as an engineer, but as a responsible citizen.
